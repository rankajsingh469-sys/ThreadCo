# 🧵 ThreadCo

### Wear your thread. Own your style.

**ThreadCo** is a modern, responsive fashion e-commerce website built to give visitors a clean and enjoyable shopping experience — from discovering products to exploring individual product details.

Built with **HTML, CSS & JavaScript**, ThreadCo focuses on responsive design, smooth interactions, and a simple fashion-first interface.

---

## ✨ What makes ThreadCo different?

🛍️ **Clean Shopping Experience**
Browse products through a simple and organized interface.

📱 **Fully Responsive**
Designed to look great across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

☰ **Smooth Mobile Navigation**
A responsive hamburger menu keeps navigation clean on smaller screens.

🎨 **Modern UI**
Minimal layouts, stylish typography, product-focused sections, and smooth transitions.

🔎 **Product Details**
Explore individual products with dedicated product-detail pages.

💬 **Contact Section**
A simple way for visitors to connect with the brand.
